# Book-shaped logo keychain

This revision follows the outline of the book and stethoscope. There is no rectangular backing plate. The backing fills the book pages and thickens the projecting stethoscope strokes slightly. An external ear above the upper-left corner holds the keyring. Its outside diameter is 9.2 mm, and its hole diameter is 4.2 mm. The original hole inside the book has been removed.

Overall dimensions are approximately 62.7 x 46.4 x 3.9 mm. Import all STLs as millimeters at 100% scale.

For easier printing, print keychain_front_glue_half.stl and keychain_qr_glue_half.stl flat-side down, artwork facing upward. Glue the flat backs together using adhesive appropriate to the print material. The outlines and ring holes align after turning the back half over. keychain_complete.stl is the single-piece alternative; inspect support and bridging requirements for its two decorated faces in your slicer.

The QR is recessed 0.5 mm into the back of the book. Modules use a 0.9824 mm pitch, with a small 0.04 mm boundary expansion to avoid point contacts. Its four-module blank margin lies within the book outline. Paint the recesses dark against a light background and keep the blank margin clean. The preview shows suggested paint colors; STL does not encode color.

The QR projection reconstructed from the exported back mesh decoded to www.thedvmauthor.com. All three meshes passed watertightness, connectivity, winding, positive-volume, and nonzero-triangle-area checks. Physical printing, strength, and scanning remain untested. Scan the finished painted part before use.


The enlarged QR pattern is approximately 24.64 mm wide. Its full footprint including the four-module quiet zone is 32.5 mm square. Placement was optimized on a 0.05 mm grid for the largest upright square within the book, with 0.5 mm additional edge clearance. It is offset to the right on the back to use the available height. The physical keychain dimensions and external ear are unchanged.
