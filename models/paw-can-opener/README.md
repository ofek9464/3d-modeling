# Paw can opener: source and current status

Original model: **Paw Can Opener by PaniCreations**, available on [MakerWorld](https://makerworld.com/en/models/2896812-paw-can-opener).

The creator's description, also embedded in the locally supplied 3MF, permits personal printing and gifts but explicitly prohibits re-uploading the model. This folder therefore contains notes only. The original geometry and the QR-edited 3MF files are not redistributed here.

## QR investigation

A local QR-edited copy was inspected after prints produced distorted QR details and inconsistent scans. The audit found that the body and paw-pad meshes were watertight, but the QR mesh was not watertight and contained disconnected components. Its digital projection decoded to `www.thedvmauthor.com`.

The inspected saved project contained a P2S 0.4 mm profile. The printer owner reported switching to an X2D profile before printing. This is a saved-project mismatch, not proof of the settings used for the failed prints.

The QR modification remains unresolved. There is no verified repaired print file in this repository. A future revision needs a closed QR mesh, suitable contrast and blank margins, slicing checks for the actual nozzle, and a physical scan test. Redistribution of any derivative containing the original model requires permission from its creator.
