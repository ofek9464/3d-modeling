# Blender cameras

Preserve the scene's intended view unless the request changes it. Choose projection, lens, framing, focus, tracking, and motion for the subject and deliverable. Reference comparison uses the registered camera convention; a cinematic preset must not replace it.

Inspect subject bounds and frame aspect ratio. Verify clipping, focus, headroom and visibility where relevant. A centered composition, rule of thirds, or shallow focus is a choice from the brief rather than a universal requirement.

Read [camera recipes](recipes.md) for framing and motion examples, or [advanced camera reference](references/overview.md) for stereo, lens effects and multiple cameras. Retarget every example to the actual subject.

Completion means the required subject is framed and focused as intended in the requested views, including representative frames when the camera moves.
